ABOUT MORPHEUS

Raised for apartment life.

Morpheus was adopted as a kitten in November 2021 and has lived with me ever since. He is a neutered, fully vaccinated indoor cat who is exceptionally clean, quiet and well trained.

Over the past five years, we have lived together in three homes across Greece, the United Kingdom and Italy. He is used to moving, settling into new spaces and respecting the home around him.

He is a little shy around new people, but once he feels safe he is sweet, calm and affectionate. His preferred daily agenda is sleeping, eating and watching birds from the window.
